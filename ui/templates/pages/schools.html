Common Questions From Principals

What schools worry about (and our answers)

"Is 3D printing safe around young children?"

Yes. For schools, we exclusively recommend PLA (Polylactic Acid) filament, which is made from biodegradable corn-starch and emits no toxic fumes or foul odors. We also supply machines with enclosed walls or protective door sensors that pause if touched during printing.

"Our teachers have zero 3D modeling experience."

Almost all teachers we train have never seen a 3D printer before! Our training uses free, browser-based tools (like Autodesk Tinkercad) that allow teachers to build geometric objects, gears, and nameplates within the first hour. We leave them with step-by-step PDF slide decks they can project directly in class.

"What are the ongoing consumable costs?"

Filament is surprisingly inexpensive: a 1kg spool costs around Rs 2,500 – Rs 3,000. Typical student projects weigh between 10 to 18 grams, which means the plastic cost per student is only Rs 30 to Rs 55! One single spool can support up to 60 to 80 student creations.

"What happens if a printer breaks mid-term?"

Unlike imported grey-market hardware where you must wait a month for parts from China, Jiddat3D maintains a dedicated inventory of replacement nozzles, thermistors, extruder gears, and heater blocks right in Karachi and Lahore. Most common clogs can be resolved in 10 minutes using the spare parts kit we include with every school lab.

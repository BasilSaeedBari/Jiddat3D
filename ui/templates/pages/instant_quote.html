Why our Instant Slicer is different

Why is there a 1.5x buffer timer applied?

3D printing in Pakistan involves real-world variables like summer ambient temperatures and UPS power transitions. We apply a 1.5x buffer so the print time you see is realistic and reliable — never an optimistic under-estimate.

How does the 1.5x weight buffer work?

When printing functional mechanical parts, we account for purge lines, support structures, and wall perimeters (1.5x safety multiplier) so you never get surprised by hidden post-processing surcharges.

Simply click the "Lock In Order on WhatsApp" button. It opens WhatsApp with your exact model dimensions, weight, and price prefilled so our Karachi workshop can immediately dispatch your invoice for prepaid confirmation.

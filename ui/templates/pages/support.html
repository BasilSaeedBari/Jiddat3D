Quick Troubleshooting Library

01

First Layer Not Sticking to Bed?

Over 90% of adhesion issues are caused by finger oils on the build plate. Wash the PEI plate in a sink with warm water and normal dish soap (e.g. Lemon Max). Dry with a clean paper towel. Do not touch the surface with bare fingers.

02

Clogged Nozzle or Clicking Extruder?

Heat your nozzle to 250°C. Use the thin acupuncture needle included in your tool kit to gently push upward into the nozzle orifice. Manually feed a short piece of PLA until plastic extrudes straight down.

03

Resuming After Load-Shedding?

When electricity returns, turn on the printer switch. The screen will prompt: "Resume interrupted print?" Clean any stray drip of plastic off the nozzle before tapping Resume so it doesn't knock the object.

04

Stringing and Cobwebs on PETG?

In humid coastal cities like Karachi, filament absorbs moisture from sea air. Dry your spool in a filament drybox or set your heated bed to 55°C with a cardboard box over the spool for 4 hours.

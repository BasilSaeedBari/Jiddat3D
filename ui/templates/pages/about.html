Our Story & Mission

We make things.
And we teach people to make them.

For too long, owning a 3D printer in Pakistan meant relying on generic imported catalogs, waiting 6 weeks for proprietary replacement parts, and struggling without local support. Jiddat3D is an engineering innovation lab designed to pioneer sovereign digital manufacturing in Pakistan.

"We do not sell ready-made, turn-key imported printers from foreign distributors. We design, calibrate, and build our own specialized 3D printing hardware in Karachi. Jiddat means innovation — sovereign hardware, open-source architectures, and an engineering ecosystem built to empower Pakistani creators."

— Basil Saeed Bari, Founder of Jiddat3D
Engineering Innovation Service

Our Hardware Horizon

Karachi R&D Facility

3D printing is just our first manufacturing frontier. As an engineering innovation lab, Jiddat3D is actively developing hardware tools to make complete desktop fabrication accessible to Pakistani universities, research labs, and product designers:

🖨️

In-House FDM/FFM Printers

Specialized, bench-assembled Cartesian & CoreXY machines engineered for Pakistan's thermal climate and 220V grid stability.

⚡

Rapid PCB Prototyping

In active roadmap: Desktop milling and chemical-free circuit prototyping services so hardware developers don't wait weeks for overseas PCB boards.

⚙️

Desktop CNC Milling

Under engineering development: Compact, high-rigidity CNC machines for precision milling of aluminum, brass, acetal, and composite carbon.

🔓
Major Commitment

100% Open Source Architecture Guarantee

We believe you should own what you purchase. If tomorrow Jiddat3D ceases to exist, your machine will never become an obsolete brick. Every machine we engineer uses standard off-the-shelf components, open Marlin or Klipper firmware configurations, transparent wiring diagrams, and publicly available CAD slicer profiles.

✓ No proprietary lock-in ✓ Fully repairable anywhere in Pakistan ✓ Free firmware configs forever

Our Core Rules

⚖️

Tell the Truth

If our entry machine fits your lab project better than a high-end CoreXY workstation, we will tell you straight. We never over-sell machine specifications.

🛠️

Be There After the Sale

A printer without spare parts is just a paperweight. We keep nozzles, build plates, belts, stepper drivers, and thermistors in stock in Karachi for immediate dispatch.

🎓

Teach, Don't Just Sell

We run hands-on workshops and train teachers because Pakistan needs creators, innovators, and hardware builders, not passive consumers.

🛡️

6-Month & STEAM Education Warranty

All Jiddat-engineered machines carry our 6-Month Working Warranty covering factory manufacturing and assembly defects under transparent bench claim clauses. Partner STEAM schools and universities receive our Dedicated Institutional Education Warranty with teacher training.

💳

Prepaid Engineering Builds Only

Because each machine is custom-assembled, bench-calibrated, and print-tested in our Karachi lab before wooden crate packing, we do not operate cash on delivery (COD). All orders are prepaid via direct bank transfer (IBFT) or Raast with official NTN invoices.

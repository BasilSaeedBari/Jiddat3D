Our Story & Mission

We make things.
And we teach people to make them.

For too long, owning a 3D printer in Pakistan meant relying on overseas catalogs, waiting 6 weeks for replacement parts, and struggling without local support. Jiddat3D was created to change that forever.

"I started building printers because I wanted affordable, sovereign manufacturing in Pakistan. I wanted engineers, students, and independent makers to own real machines — not just admire them in imported catalogs. Jiddat means innovation — a deliberate departure from the old consumer way of doing things."

— Basil Saeed Bari, Founder of Jiddat3D

Our Core Rules

⚖️

Tell the Truth

If a cheaper printer fits your project better than a high-end model, we will tell you straight. We never over-sell machine specs.

🛠️

Be There After the Sale

A printer without spare parts is just a paperweight. We keep nozzles, build plates, belts, and thermistors in stock in Pakistan.

🎓

Teach, Don't Just Sell

We run hands-on workshops and train teachers because Pakistan needs creators and builders, not passive consumers.

Visit Our Karachi Workshop

Located in DHA Phase 1, Karachi. See machines in live operation, test our filament, or discuss your school lab project in person (appointment recommended).

Engineering, Architecture & CS Students

Final Year Project (FYP) Print Clinic

Every year, hundreds of engineering students at NED, NUST, UET, FAST, and GIKI lose marks because of broken prototypes or bad print tolerances.

Bring your CAD design to Jiddat3D. Our engineers run a free pre-print check: we verify wall thicknesses, ensure bolt holes won't crack under load, select the right infill geometry, and print parts ready for your defense jury.

Book an FYP Print Consultation

What we check before printing your FYP:

01

Wall Thickness & Shells: Prevent flimsy parts from cracking when motors or bearings are tightened.

02

Hole Shrinkage Tolerances: Adjust M3/M4 screw holes so hardware fits tightly without drilling.

03

Stress-Oriented Layer Alignment: Rotate parts so mechanical force doesn't shear layer adhesion.

04

Heat & Load Suitability: Switch from PLA to PETG/ABS for parts exposed to engine bay heat or UV sun.
